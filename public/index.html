Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What this site is

This is a small, independent song-request tool built for live events. It's not a company product - there's no ad tracking, no data resale, and no account system beyond what's described below.

What we collect

  • The name you type in when you make your first request. It's shown to the DJ/admin so they know who requested each song - that's the only reason it's collected.
  • A random anonymous ID, generated in your browser and stored locally on your device, used only to stop the same device from voting on a song more than once. It isn't linked to your name or any personal info.
  • Your song requests and votes themselves (title, artist, timestamp), so the queue can function and the DJ can see request history for the event.

If you use "Connect Spotify"

This is optional and only used to let you browse your own Spotify playlists to find songs to request. When you connect, your browser talks directly to Spotify - your login and access token never pass through or get stored on this site's server. The token is kept temporarily in your browser (session storage) and is automatically discarded when it expires or you close your browser.

What we don't do

  • We don't require an account, email, or password to use this site.
  • We don't sell, share, or use your data for advertising.
  • We don't use tracking cookies. The only browser storage used is described above, and it's functional, not for tracking you across other sites.

How long data is kept

Requests, votes, and names are kept only for the duration of the event and are cleared when the server restarts between events. Nothing here is stored in a long-term database.

Third parties

Song search and playback use the Spotify API. Spotify's own handling of your data (if you log in) is covered by Spotify's Privacy Policy, not this one.

Questions

This site is made and maintained by Sam B. If you have questions about how it works, ask whoever's running the event tonight, or reach out directly if you know how to find them.
