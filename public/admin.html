Spotify Queue Relay

When connected, every newly-accepted request is automatically added to your live Spotify app queue. This logs in as your own Spotify account (not guests') and requires Spotify Premium plus an active device (open Spotify and play something first). Click to (re)connect.

Fallback Playlist

Switches Spotify to this playlist immediately - whatever was queued up next from the old one is replaced. If a request happens to be playing right now, this will interrupt it, so best used between songs. Guest requests you've explicitly queued aren't affected by this - they still get added to the queue as normal and play before anything from a playlist.

Kiosk Page (kiosk.html)

Independent settings for a DJ-attended kiosk device - separate on/off, Spotify button, and credit rules from regular guests. Each physical kiosk still gets its own credit bank, same as any guest's phone.

Display-Only Mode turns the kiosk page into a full-screen, read-only "now playing" board - no search, no credits, no voting, just the Live Queue blown up big. Good for a TV or monitor at the venue instead of a device guests interact with directly.

Danger Zone

Permanently deletes this event and everything in it - queue, history, settings, Spotify connection. This can't be undone, and the URL becomes free for anyone to reuse.
