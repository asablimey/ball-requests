One catalog for ALL events. Loading...

This table is shared by every event, past and future; nothing here belongs to a single event slug. Each cell saves when you click away. Paste a full Spotify or YouTube link into a cell and it becomes the ID. You can also paste a block copied from your Google Sheet: it fills rows downward from the cell you paste into, in the column order shown (a pasted header row is skipped). A row only needs Title, Artist and the YouTube link/ID. The player recognises the song by Title + Artist (ignoring things like "- Remastered" or "(feat. X)"); Spotify ID and ISRC are optional and only used to pin an exact version. No row, or Video OFF, means ambient visuals. Start time is how far into the YouTube video the song begins. NSFW: with Family mode on, only rows marked NO play (blank is treated as not safe).
